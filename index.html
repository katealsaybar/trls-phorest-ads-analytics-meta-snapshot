Key findings for Morph
CPA collapsed from ~AED 7 to AED 34 from March — directly tied to pausing Performance Max. Restoring or rebuilding this campaign should be priority #1.
Dubai campaigns are 3× more expensive than Abu Dhabi. Hair Services Dubai CPA = AED 40 vs Abu Dhabi's AED 13. Needs a dedicated Dubai strategy.
Paid Search drives 39% of sessions but has the lowest engagement rate (48%) and only 24s avg. session time — landing page is the likely conversion leak.
91% of spend hits mobile users. With only 24s avg. engagement, the mobile landing experience needs urgent review before scaling budget.
Organic Search is highest quality traffic: 71% engagement rate, 38s avg. session. SEO is quietly working — worth protecting.
Paid Social has the longest avg. session time (40s) despite low volume. High-intent audience — underinvested channel for Dubai acquisition.
881 form submits recorded — this is the conversion anchor. Morph needs to map this to actual bookings to validate CPA quality.
Audience targeting is solid: 96% female, 25–44 core demo = 66%. Focus budget and creative, not targeting.
